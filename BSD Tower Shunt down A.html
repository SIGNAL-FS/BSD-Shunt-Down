Bishan Depot (BSD) Shutdown Procedure Checklist

BISHAN DEPOT (BSD) CONTROL TOWER
Power Maintenance Works

PURPOSE

  • During Power Maintenance works, BSD Control Tower might experience a shutdown for certain periods
  • All equipment will draw power from UPS (Uninterrupted Power Supply)
  • When restoring power back to main supply, Tower may experience surge in current
  • This will result in certain equipment/track circuits to fail
  • Must be well-prepared to act fast to restore equipment before first train launch

Note: The steps listed in this checklist are specifically for BSD Control Tower

PREPARATION BEFORE WORKS - SPARES

Ensure the following spares are topped up before the shutdown:

✓ Item Location/Notes
3A fuse for location case
8A fuse for location case
Different fuse types for SER
Filament lamp for signal light
Legacy PSU
PLC modules
CBTC Enable Relay for Control Tower

PREPARATION WORK

  • Confirm the work schedule with Power team beforehand
  • Ensure minimum 2 manpower for standby
  • Equipment is on L3, L4, and L5 – sufficient to split if necessary
  • PRIORITY: Always restore/shutdown L3 first

PRECAUTIONS DURING WORK

⚠️ WARNING: DO NOT take the lift in the control tower close to the shutdown period mentioned by the POW staff. Might potentially be stuck inside when power goes out.

Ensure that the POW staff keep you informed prior to the shutdown.

SHUTDOWN SEQUENCE - OVERALL STEPS

Sequence: Start with L3 first, then move onto L4 and L5

Overall Shutdown Order:

  1. CBTC Equipment (Steps 1–4)
  2. Legacy Equipment (Steps 5–9)

CBTC EQUIPMENT SHUTDOWN

STEP #1 — Switch off SRS 1 / SRS 2 / DR Server PC & Diag Server PC (Remote via NMS), then Switch off CB

Part A: Remote Shutdown via NMS

  • 📍 Level 3 (L3): Switch off SRS 1, SRS 2, and DR Server PC remotely from NMS
  • 📍 Level 4 (L4): Switch off Diag Server PC remotely from NMS
  • 🔶 Verification: Check indication light turns steady Orange on each server before proceeding to power off.

Part B: Switch off CB (Circuit Breaker)

After confirming all servers are shut down remotely, physically switch off the CB located at the back top of the following racks:

  • 📍 ATS/DCS Rack: Switch off CB at back top of rack
  • 📍 Diag Rack: Switch off CB at back top of rack
⚠️ IMPORTANT: Ensure all servers are fully shut down via NMS BEFORE switching off the CB. Do NOT switch off CB while servers are still running.
