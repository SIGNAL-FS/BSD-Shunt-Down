Power Up Procedure

REVERSE SEQUENCE RESTORATION

Restore power to BSD Control Tower equipment in the reverse order of shutdown to prevent damage from power surges and ensure stable restoration before first train launch.

  • Main Power & PLC Equipment (Steps 1–2)
  • Legacy Equipment Power Up (Steps 3–6)
  • CBTC Equipment Boot Sequence (Steps 7–12)
  • NMS link verification integrated

⚠ Important Safety Reminders

  • DO NOT take the lift in the control tower close to the shutdown or power restoration period — you may get stuck inside.
  • Ensure minimum 2 manpower on standby for equipment verification.
  • Verify all spare parts (fuses, PLC modules, PSU, relays) are readily available before starting.
  • Always coordinate with Power Team (POW staff) before switching equipment.
  • Priority: Always restore Level 3 first, then L4 and L5.
