BISHAN DEPOT (BSD) CONTROL TOWER

Power Up Procedure Checklist (Reverse Sequence)

Power Restoration Works

PURPOSE

During Power Maintenance works, BSD Control Tower experiences a shutdown. When restoring power back to the main supply, the Tower may experience a surge in current. This will result in certain equipment/track circuits to fail.

CRITICAL: Equipment must be powered up in the REVERSE ORDER of the shutdown sequence to prevent damage from power surges and ensure stable restoration before the first train launch.

PREPARATION BEFORE POWER UP

  • Confirm with Power team that maintenance is complete and power is ready to be restored.
  • Ensure minimum 2 manpower are on standby for equipment verification.
  • Verify all spare parts are readily available in case of surge damage:
    • 3A & 8A fuses for location case
    • Different fuse types for SER
    • Filament lamps for signal lights
    • Legacy PSU, PLC modules, CBTC Enable Relay
⚠️ PRECAUTION: DO NOT take the lift in the control tower close to the power restoration period. You might potentially be stuck inside when power is switched. Ensure POW staff keep you informed prior to energizing.

POWER UP SEQUENCE - OVERALL STEPS

PRIORITY: Always restore L3 first, then move onto L4 and L5.
OVERALL POWER UP ORDER: Final 415V Switchboard ➔ PLC Racks ➔ Legacy Equipment ➔ CBTC Equipment (SRS/LAN/Diag/DR)

Note: Sequence is strictly reversed from the Shutdown Procedure, with specific NMS link checks integrated into the CBTC boot sequence.

MAIN POWER & PLC EQUIPMENT POWER UP

STEP #1 (Reverse of Final Step #11): Switch ON the 415V Switchboard for CBTC and Legacy Equipment (located beside the PDP panel).

STEP #2 (Reverse of Step #10): Move to the PLC rack. Turn ON ALL the links.

LEGACY EQUIPMENT POWER UP

STEP #3 (Reverse of Step #6): Switch ON the MJ80 Point Machine Power Switchboard (415V).

STEP #4 (Reverse of Step #7): Move to the AC/DC power section. Switch ON in this REVERSE order:

  1. #2 (MAIN INCOMER)
  2. #1 (240V A.C. SECTION)

STEP #5 (Reverse of Step #9): Move to the MAIN INCOMER section. Turn ON the breaker.

STEP #6 (Reverse of Step #8): In the 240V A.C. SECTION, refer to the original configuration photo. Switch ON the breakers from BIGGEST to SMALLEST:

CBTC EQUIPMENT POWER UP (ATS/DCS RACK)

STEP #7 (Reverse of Step #5): Move to the PDP panel. Refer to the "BEFORE" configuration photo taken during shutdown. Turn ON ALL breakers one by one to their original states.

STEP #8 (SRS1 & LAN 1 Initialization): Power ON the SRS1 PC and LAN Switch 1.

  • SRS1 PC Powered ON
  • LAN Switch 1 Powered ON
⚠️ CRITICAL CHECK: Check with NMS (Network Management System) to confirm the link is UP before proceeding to the next step.

STEP #9 (SRS2 & LAN 2 Initialization): Power ON the SRS2 PC and LAN Switch 2.

  • SRS2 PC Powered ON
  • LAN Switch 2 Powered ON

STEP #10 (Diag PC): Power ON the Diag PC.

  • Diag PC Powered ON

STEP #11 (Reverse of Step #3 - DR PC): Power ON the DR PC.

  • DR PC Powered ON

STEP #12 (Reverse of Step #1 & #2 - Final Boot): ATS/DCS Rack: Turn ON any remaining PCs / Initiate full System Boot-up using the switches at the back.

* This concludes the switching ON of equipment on Level 3.

POST POWER-UP VERIFICATION & COMPLETION CHECKLIST

  • All equipment on L3 has been powered up and verified (Repeat for L4 & L5 if applicable).
  • NMS confirms all network links are UP and stable.
  • Checked for any track circuit failures or equipment faults caused by power surge.
  • Replaced any blown fuses or failed modules using standby spares.
  • Verified CBTC and Legacy systems are fully operational and communicating.
  • POW staff / Control Center has been notified of successful power restoration.
  • Tower is cleared and ready for first train launch.
